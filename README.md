# Dapur Ngebul — Best Cooking Game

Run a fast-food warung for one 150-second shift. Customers arrive with orders on the board (max 3, each with a patience bar). Cook 4 recipes — Nasi Goreng, Mie Goreng, Sate Ayam, Gado-Gado — by grabbing ingredients (click or keys 1–6), chopping (2.5s), cooking (4–5s), and assembling on the serving table. Dishes left sitting burn after 4 seconds and must be binned. Serve the right customer before their patience runs out; 3 walk-outs end the shift early. Score is dish price plus tips scaled by patience and combo (up to x2). Pause with P, mute with M.

Plays offline in a desktop browser from its own files — open `index.html` (or serve the folder) and press start. All art is drawn on canvas and all sound is synthesized with WebAudio; there are no network requests. `main.ts` is the entrypoint; the game lives in `game/`.
